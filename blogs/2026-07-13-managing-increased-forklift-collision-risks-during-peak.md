Peak seasons and periods of sustained high throughput place exceptional demands on warehouse and industrial operations worldwide. Increased order volumes, extended shifts, temporary workforce expansion, and rapid layout adjustments create conditions where forklift collision risks rise significantly. Industry benchmarks highlight the scale: forklifts were associated with 84 fatalities in the US in 2024 and an estimated 35,000–62,000 injuries annually.
