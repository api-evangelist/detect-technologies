Forklifts are indispensable to modern warehouse operations, yet they remain one of the most persistent safety risks. In the United States alone, forklifts are associated with tens of thousands of injuries and numerous fatalities each year, with warehousing and logistics environments accounting for a significant share. Most of these incidents are preventable only if organizations move beyond reactive safety practices toward real-time, intelligence-driven interventions.
